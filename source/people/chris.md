  Chris Cotropia is the Board President and Dennis I. Belcher Professor of Law at the University of Richmond School of Law. He writes in the areas of empirical legal studies, patent law, and intellectual property. He is the author of numerous book chapters, amicus briefs, and law review articles on these subjects. Prior to joining the Richmond Law faculty, Cotropia was the C.J. Morrow Research Professor of Law at Tulane University School of Law
